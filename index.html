What are Google Play’s current personal-account testing requirements?

For personal developer accounts created after November 13, 2023, Google requires at least 12 testers opted in continuously for the previous 14 days before you can apply for production access. You also answer questions about testing and app readiness. Meeting the count alone does not grant automatic approval.

Google’s official testing requirements
Private testing or free mutual testing: what changes?

With free mutual testing in Telegram, you install other developers’ apps and complete daily check-ins to receive reciprocal testing. Private testing costs $20 with crypto or $23 with PayPal or bank transfer: we coordinate 12+ testers through a 15-day cycle, with no reciprocal tasks for you.

Why 15 days if Google asks for 14?

Our managed service includes an extra operational buffer day around counter updates and time-zone cutoffs in Play Console. It gives the cycle breathing room; it does not change Google’s 14-day minimum or guarantee a production decision.

Real people and devices, or emulators and install farms?

Private testing uses real people on physical Android phones. We do not sell a counter of idle installs. Google may require more testing when engagement is insufficient. Real testing helps you find issues; your app must still meet Google Play policies, and no testing service can remove that responsibility.

Do I need to collect every tester’s email?

No. Add our Google Group as a tester group in Play Console: google-play-dev-test@googlegroups.com. Then enable all countries, send your changes for review and share the “Join on Android” testing link. The wizard includes screenshots for every setup step.

Can testers install my paid app without buying it?

Yes, after you configure License testing. In Play Console → Settings → License testing, choose Google Groups, add google-play-dev-test@googlegroups.com, keep RESPOND_NORMALLY and save. This is separate from adding the group to your closed testing track.

What if Google rejects my production access application?

We stay with you: review the feedback with our support team, correct testing setup where needed and plan the next testing steps. App changes and policy issues may need work from you. Our commitment is testing support through the application process; Google alone decides production access.

Do I have to give you access to my Play Console?

No passwords, admin invitations or console access are needed. You keep control of your developer account. Add the Google Group yourself and send the app name and closed testing link.

How do I pay and track the test?

Pay $20 by internal crypto exchange transfer, or $23 with PayPal or a bank transfer. For bank details, first create a requisites request and contact @garantXchange. Upload the payment receipt, then track your order in the website account. Telegram notifications require permission for the bot to message you. Detailed engagement is visible in your Play Console.

Why does Telegram sometimes ask for my phone number?

The Login Widget authorizes your browser, separately from the accounts installed in Telegram apps. A different browser, private tab or app clone may have a separate login session. If Telegram requests a number, enter the account you want on oauth.telegram.org and confirm in that Telegram account. DevTestHub never receives your phone number or login code.
