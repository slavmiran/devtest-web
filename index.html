The participant code

Four agreements. Read them before you take the first project.

  1. Stay until the agreed end

    Closed testing — 14+ days · Published apps — the period you agreed

    Your own project can already be in review. Exchanges you accepted still run until each of them ends. Leaving early drops Reliability. Complaints and violations can close the account or remove the project.

    If the project needs an extension

    The owner lengthens the cycle and reserves $BUST for the team, so the exchange stays even. End dates can drift apart because of that.

  2. Check the app, don’t just open it

    Find a bug, leave a recommendation, attach the checkpoint screens. That is the useful part. The phone model is set once in the profile.

  3. Honest review, inside and out

    We don’t back fake store reviews. If the app really earned it, a public review is your choice. For the developer, leave an internal one: Ease of use & interface, Stability & speed, Usefulness & features.

  4. Build a reputation people can see

    Reliability, Karma and Contribution stay on the profile and decide who takes your project next. Active members get more ways to grow their own apps.

Questions before you start

Choose the question closest to the decision you are making.

Who needs 12 testers for 14 days?

For personal Google Play developer accounts created after November 13, 2023, at least 12 testers must stay opted in continuously for the 14 days before you apply. Once that condition is met, you can request production access in Play Console. Google reviews the application.

Google Play testing requirements
How does mutual testing differ from private testing?

In a free exchange, developers use each other’s builds and check in daily. With private testing, DevTestHub coordinates 12 or more testers on real Android devices. You follow the work without taking on partner apps.

Why plan for 14+ days?

The minimum opt-in period is continuous. We usually schedule a full 15 days for a managed test, allowing a buffer for time-zone boundaries and Play Console updates. In an exchange, keep each partner’s agreed end date.

What does the $75 review add to the basic test?

The basic test covers team activity and help answering the production-access questionnaire. It costs $23 with crypto, PayPal or bank transfer. The $75 total includes a written app review with device coverage, reproducible issues and a prioritized fix plan. Arrange it with the team in Telegram.

How do I connect testers to my build?

Add google-play-dev-test@googlegroups.com to the tester groups for your closed track. Make the build available in the required regions, send the changes for review and share the Android testing link. You keep sole control of your Play Console.

Can the team test a paid app?

Add the tester group in Play Console’s License testing settings as well as in the closed track. Keep the license response at RESPOND_NORMALLY. The setup guide shows both places.

What if Google asks for more testing?

Send us the review response. We continue testing until you receive production access or refund the service fee. You handle any code or policy changes Google requests. Google makes the final access decision.

Why does the bot use Telegram Stars?

Telegram requires Stars for digital services sold inside bots and Mini Apps. Choose Stars there, or use crypto, PayPal or bank transfer on this website. You can attach a receipt and follow the order in your account.

Can I join with an app that is already published?

Yes. List the app in the Mini App and choose a task for other developers to try. They can assess clarity, ease of use and stability, document a bug or suggest a change. Public store reviews are always voluntary.

What does a growth coordinator take on?

A Managing Coordinator is meant to run mutual commitments, team discipline and report quality on a published app. You keep the project and judge the result in Play Console or the bot. Choosing coordinators and connecting projects are still in development. Until then, grow the app with other developers.

How to read a profile

Keeps the agreement. Shows up and finishes every exchange. A miss or an early exit drops the number.
Karma
How people see you, not a click counter. Ratings, thanks and notes on your profile. Members and moderation move it.
Contribution
Work past the daily check-in. Bugs, recommendations, reviews, screenshot series, finished scenarios, video and comments that actually move someone’s app.
