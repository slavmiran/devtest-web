FAQ

A few things to know.

What is included in private testing?

12+ real testers on physical Android devices, a managed 15-day closed testing cycle, and help preparing your production access application. You do not need to test other apps.

How much does it cost?

One app costs $20 paid by internal crypto exchange transfer, or $23 with PayPal or bank transfer, including a $3 processing fee.

Can I test a paid Android app?

Yes. Add google-play-dev-test@googlegroups.com in Google Play Console License testing and keep RESPOND_NORMALLY so testers can install without buying the app.

Does DevTestHub guarantee Google Play approval?

Google makes the final production access and publishing decisions. DevTestHub manages testing and helps prepare your application; testing is not a substitute for meeting Google Play policies.

Is there a free testing option?

Yes. Open @Android12TestersBot in Telegram to join mutual testing. You test other developers’ apps and they test yours.

Where can I track my order and receive my receipt?

Sign in with Telegram to see your active orders here. Allow messages from the bot or start it to receive your receipt in Telegram. Bank payment details are arranged with @garantXchange.
